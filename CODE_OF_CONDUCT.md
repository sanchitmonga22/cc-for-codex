# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led. Assume good intent, critique code and decisions rather than people, and make the project welcoming to contributors regardless of background or experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publication of another person's credentials or proprietary code are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community and the project's users.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at [sanchitmonga22@gmail.com](mailto:sanchitmonga22@gmail.com). For security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
